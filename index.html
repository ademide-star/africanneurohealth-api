// What This App Does

AI-Powered Brain Health Screening,
Built for African Communities

The African NeuroHealth Dashboard is a free, AI-driven platform that predicts your risk of stroke and dementia in minutes. Our machine learning models — externally validated on an independent African cohort — combine biomedical measurements, lifestyle habits, environmental exposures, and cognitive testing to give you a personalised risk score with clear, actionable next steps.

Beyond risk prediction, the platform includes a nutrition tracker, a stress assessment built around African stress domains, a memory recall game, and an instant PDF report generated in your language — English, Yorùbá, Hausa, Igbo, French, Arabic, or Swahili. No login is required to get started.

For researchers, the same platform doubles as live research infrastructure: upload and export datasets, visualise stroke and dementia hotspots across Africa on NeuroMap, and browse a documented ethnobotanical database of herbal remedies — all built on real field data collected through community assessments.

🔬 Preclinical Evidence

Saigon Cinnamon — Laboratory Evidence

26 biomarkers · 84 Wistar rats · 7 treatment groups · Male & Female
Neuroscience & Inflammation Unit, University of Ilorin · Adebimpe-John et al. (in prep.)

📊 Population Context (ANHRL Survey, n=4,000, Ogun State): Among 500 community members reporting Saigon/Ceylon cinnamon use, hypertension rates (25.2%) and forgetting symptoms were slightly lower than non-herb users (26.8%), though not statistically significant (p=0.96). This reflects food-level spice use, not therapeutic dosing. The mechanistic evidence above comes from controlled preclinical research at pharmacological doses. Human dose-response studies are needed before clinical recommendations can be made.
