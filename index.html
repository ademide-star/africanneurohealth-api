// About the Lab

Where African Neuroscience
Meets Artificial Intelligence

The African NeuroHealth Dashboard is a culturally attuned, context-aware digital health platform developed to assess neuro-health risks within African populations. It integrates conventional biomedical indicators with locally relevant environmental exposures, lifestyle behaviors, dietary patterns, traditional medicine use, and sociocultural determinants to provide comprehensive, personalized, and precision-driven neuro-health risk assessments.

The platform incorporates environmental exposures such as noise and air pollution, evaluates dietary habits including traditional African nutrition, assesses sleep quality and hydration status, documents the use of herbal and traditional remedies, measures psychosocial stressors unique to African settings, and captures ethnocultural identity to enhance population-specific precision health insights.

Designed to support research, clinical decision-making, and community health screening, the African NeuroHealth Dashboard leverages artificial intelligence and data-driven analytics to facilitate early identification of individuals at risk for stroke, cognitive impairment, and other neurological disorders across African communities.
